Cordaville, Fayville and the rest of Southborough

Southborough is a Worcester County town west of Framingham, and its villages include Cordaville and Fayville. Fayville has its own ZIP, 01745, while most of the town uses 01772. Tell the dispatcher which village you are in so the crew can find the right road quickly.

A town with more than one village and a long run of rural roads means addresses can be hard to find at night. Give the crew your house number, the road name and the nearest crossing road, and keep an outside light on.

When water is spreading in Southborough

Our dispatch line is answered around the clock. A burst pipe on a winter night is handled the same way as a leak on a weekday afternoon.

While the crew is on the way, close the main valve, keep power off to wet areas when the panel is dry, and lift valuables out of the water. If gas or sparking outlets are involved, call 911 before you call us.

Town water comes from the MWRA

Southborough buys all of its water from the Massachusetts Water Resources Authority. The town's Department of Public Works runs the Water Division from 147 Cordaville Road, with two pumping stations and three water tanks distributing it. Public Works is open weekdays, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., at 508-485-1210.

The practical point is who to call. A break in the street main is the town's job, and the town lists its police line, 508-485-2147, for after-hours contact. A burst pipe after your meter is yours to shut off, and we handle the drying.

If water is still spreading in Southborough

Do these in order while the crew is on the road.

1

Isolate the water

Close the main valve, then check outdoor spigots and the water heater supply for any other open line.

2

Stay out of live circuits

If the wet area includes outlets or a basement panel, wait for the utility. Do not stand in water near electrical equipment.

3

Lift and block

Raise furniture legs on blocks and move boxes to shelves. Roll back area rugs and pull up loose items.

4

Light the way

Turn on exterior lights, clear the driveway and be ready to point out the damage and the shutoff.

Interstate 90, Route 9 and the Sudbury Reservoir

Crews reach Southborough from Framingham by Route 9 or by Interstate 90, about seven miles to the west. Sudbury Reservoir lies within the town, and homes near large water bodies deserve a look at the FEMA flood map.

Check your address on the FEMA map and read your policy for a flood exclusion. After heavy rain, a wet basement near the reservoir area may be a flood claim, and standard homeowners policies do not cover flood.
