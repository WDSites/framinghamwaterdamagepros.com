Water damage in Nobscot homes and apartments

The City of Framingham describes Nobscot as a community in the northwest part of the city. The city says postwar development added single-family subdivisions and apartment buildings, and that many homes and public buildings from the 1700s and 1800s remain. That mix means a water problem in Nobscot can start in a ranch-style house, a multi-unit building or a much older home, and each is dried differently.

Nobscot is part of the Framingham service area. This page explains what to do when water gets in and how a drying job is handled.

Where Nobscot sits

According to the city, the center of Nobscot is Nobscot Village, at the meeting of Edgell Road, Water Street and Edmands Road. The village has shops and services and sits among the residential streets around it. We do not draw a boundary for the neighborhood, because the city has not published one, and we do not tie it to a single ZIP code. Tell the dispatcher your street and the nearest cross street and we will take it from there.

Do not confuse this Nobscot with Nobscot Hill and the Boy Scout reservation across the line in Sudbury. This page covers the Framingham neighborhood only.

What to do in the first hour

Follow these in order and only if it is safe.

1

Stop the source

Close the main water valve for a pipe or appliance leak. In an apartment, tell the building manager or landlord right away.

2

Keep away from electricity

Do not use the breaker panel, outlets or extension cords while standing in water or if the area is wet. Call 911 or the utility about any electrical or gas problem.

3

Do not dry sewage yourself

Water from a drain, toilet or sewer line is contaminated. Keep people and pets out and wait for a trained crew.

4

Photograph first

Take photos and video before you move anything, then lift what you can onto dry surfaces.

How extraction and drying work

We check walls, floors and ceilings with a moisture meter, including areas that look dry. Standing water is extracted first. Then air movers and dehumidifiers go in, and we take repeat readings until the materials reach their dry standard. Water mitigation explains each stage.

In an apartment building, water often travels to the unit below, so the plan may involve more than one home. We explain the scope and the price before work begins. If drywall, insulation or flooring has to be removed, the rebuild is covered under water damage repair.

Mold wording we use

Materials that stay wet can grow mold, so quick drying is the best protection. If mold has already formed, we offer mold remediation. We make no health claims about mold. Anyone who is worried about their health should speak to a doctor.

Insurance documentation

Photograph the damage, keep receipts, and write down when the water started and what caused it. Generally, sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, gradual leaks often are not, and flood is usually excluded from a standard homeowners policy. Renters should ask about renter's coverage, and landlords and owners should check what their policy requires. We cannot promise what a claim will pay.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified for Nobscot. To check flood risk at your address, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask your insurer about flood coverage.
