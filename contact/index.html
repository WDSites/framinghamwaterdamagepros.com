What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help a crew arrive with the right equipment. You do not need all of them to call.

  • Where the water is coming from, if you know
  • What it has touched, such as carpet, drywall or electrical
  • How long it has been there
  • Your street and ZIP code
  • Whether anyone has shut off the main water valve
