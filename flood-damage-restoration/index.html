Flood water is an emergency from the first minute

Flood water carries soil, bacteria and whatever else it crossed on the way in, so it is handled as contaminated until proven otherwise. Our dispatch line is open 24/7. Call as soon as water is in the house and we will arrange a crew, aiming for the same day when one is available.

Until they arrive, keep people and pets out of the water, stay away from the electrical panel if the floor is wet, and do not run a household vacuum on it. Lift anything valuable that you can reach safely and photograph the waterline on the walls before anything is moved.

If the water is in contact with outlets or the panel, call the utility or 911 first. A restoration crew cannot go into a room with live electrical hazards.

How flooding happens in Framingham

The Sudbury River runs through the city, and Sucker Brook feeds into the neighborhoods around Walnut Street. The city's hazard planning names the river and the brook as sources of flooding, and a federal grant paid for a study of flooding along Walnut Street. A later state grant of $269,030 funded design and permitting to increase the capacity of Sucker Brook, with construction funding still to be sought.

The Sudbury River drains a large basin that includes Ashland, Southborough, Wayland and Sudbury. After the heavy March rains of 2010, the U.S. Geological Survey recorded record or near-record peaks at 13 of 37 streamgages in central and eastern Massachusetts. Saturated ground is the common thread, because it leaves nowhere for rain to go but into basements.

To see where your own home sits, search your address on the FEMA flood map service center. The 100-year floodplain is mapped from FEMA data, and the city's 2022 hazard plan update lists riverine flooding, dam failures and ice jams as flood hazards.

Flood water compared with a clean leak

The cause changes the plan, the cost and the insurance picture. This table sets out the main differences.

Clean leak (Category 1)Flood or sewage (Category 3)
Typical sourceSupply line, appliance, water heaterRiver, brook, storm runoff, sewer backup
MaterialsOften dried in place if caught quicklyPorous materials usually removed
Crew protectionStandardProtective gear, containment where needed
Typical cost driversDrying days and equipmentRemoval, disposal, sanitizing and drying
Insurance, in general termsOften covered if sudden and accidentalUsually excluded from standard homeowners policies

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance states that standard homeowners policies do not cover flood damage. Flood insurance through the National Flood Insurance Program or a private insurer is separate.

Basement flooding cleanup

A flooded basement is the most common flood call. The water has to come out, then the walls, slab and anything stored below the waterline have to be assessed. Finished basements add drywall, insulation and carpet to the list, and every one of them wicks water upward.

Crews pump or extract the water, remove contaminated carpet and pad, cut drywall back above the wicking line and set drying equipment. Concrete slabs and block walls dry slowly, so the monitoring period is longer than in a framed room.

Water that came up from a drain or in from outside is not the same as water from a pipe. Mention where it came from on the call so the crew arrives with the right gear. For what to do in the first two days, the emergency water removal team and this crew work together.

Raw sewage from a basement drain: what to do first

If raw sewage is coming up through a floor drain, toilet or shower, treat it as a safety problem before a cleanup problem. Do not use the plumbing, stay out of the water, keep children and pets away, and do not try to clean it with household products.

Call the city Department of Public Works if the blockage may be in the main line, since a restoration crew cannot fix a municipal backup. Then call us to handle what the sewage touched. Porous materials such as carpet, pad and drywall that sewage reached are removed, hard surfaces are cleaned and sanitized, and the area is dried.

Insurance for sewer and drain backup is usually tied to a separate endorsement or a flood policy. The Division of Insurance advises checking your declarations page for a water backup or sump pump failure endorsement.
