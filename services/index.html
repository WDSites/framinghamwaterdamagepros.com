How the services fit together

A single water loss usually needs more than one service, in a set order. Water is stopped and removed first, the structure is dried, ruined materials are cleaned out and the room is rebuilt. Mold work joins the chain only when moisture has fed growth.

If you are not sure which page to read, start with the situation. Water is still spreading: emergency water removal and water mitigation. Water came in from outside: flood damage restoration. The water has stopped and wet material remains: water damage cleanup. The structure is dry and needs rebuilding: water damage repair. You see or smell growth: mold remediation.
