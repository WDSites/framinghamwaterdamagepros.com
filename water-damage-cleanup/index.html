The first 48 hours after the water stops

Once the source is shut off, the clock keeps running. Wet drywall, carpet and cardboard begin to smell within a day or two, and contaminated water gets worse the longer it sits. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, and cleanup crews are sent the same day when they are available.

While you wait, open windows if weather allows, run a fan only on clean-water spills and keep children and pets out of affected rooms. Do not use a household vacuum on wet floors. Wear gloves and boots if you have to walk through the area, and wash hands afterward.

What cleanup means after water damage

Cleanup is the phase between stopping the water and rebuilding. It means removing materials that cannot be dried, cleaning and sanitizing surfaces, handling your belongings and controlling odor. It follows water mitigation, which stops the damage and dries the structure, and it comes before water damage repair, which rebuilds.

On many jobs the three overlap. The crew that pumps and dries also removes wet drywall and carpet pad, and the same visit may include cleaning. What matters is that the order is followed so nothing gets rebuilt over wet material.

Sanitizing and odor control

Water that sat in a house leaves a smell even after the visible damage is gone. Crews clean surfaces, apply antimicrobial treatment where the water category calls for it and use air scrubbers with HEPA filtration to pull particles out of the air.

Odors tied to wet materials usually clear once those materials are out and the structure is dry. Masking sprays only cover the smell for a few days. If an odor returns after cleanup, tell the crew, because it can point to moisture still trapped in a cavity.

What to keep for your insurance claim

Large losses need more paperwork. A written record starts with what you do on day one.

  • ✓Receipts for emergency costs like pumping, hotel stays and equipment.
  • ✓The date and time you noticed the water and when you called for help.

Why cleanup timing depends on the water source

A line failure inside the house starts out clean and then picks up dirt as it crosses flooring and insulation. After about a day or two it should be treated with more caution than it looked at first.

Flood water from a brook or river, or sewage from a drain, is contaminated from the start. The Division of Insurance notes that flood damage is generally excluded from standard homeowners policies, so find out which kind of loss you have before cleanup decisions are final.
